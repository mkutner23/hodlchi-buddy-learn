# Hodlchi — Seed Deck Outline (six-month pitch)

Working outline for a 10–12 slide seed deck. Optimized for a narrative built around **the relationship, not the curriculum**.

---

## Slide 1 — Cover

- Hodlchi logo + Penny.
- One line: _"A virtual pet whose life gets better as your financial life gets better."_
- Founder, date, contact.

## Slide 2 — The insight

- People don't fail at money because they lack information. They fail because nothing in their life is _pulling them_ toward the small daily behaviors that compound.
- Duolingo proved: a companion that depends on you beats a course every time.
- No one has done this for money.

## Slide 3 — What Hodlchi is

- Meet Penny (GIF: egg → hatch → wave → first lesson → evolve).
- 5-minute daily lessons feed Penny. Penny remembers, reacts, evolves, misses you.
- The user isn't studying finance. The user is raising someone who happens to make them financially confident.

## Slide 4 — Why now

- Financial literacy is a stated priority for Gen Z + parents of Gen Z.
- Duolingo's public numbers proved that "companion-driven habit" scales past $8B in market cap.
- Generative AI makes a genuinely responsive companion cheap for the first time.
- Category is empty. No pet-first finance product exists at consumer scale.

## Slide 5 — The moat

- We are not defensible because of lessons. We are defensible because of the bond.
- A 60-day Penny is not replaceable. Switching costs are emotional, not technical.
- Every day of use widens the moat automatically.

## Slide 6 — Product principles

- Relationship over features.
- Feelings ship, not features.
- Warm, never needy.
- Product decision test: _"Does this make someone more likely to care about Penny?"_

## Slide 7 — Early evidence (six months in)

- Private beta cohort size, D1 / D7 retention, evolution-completion rate.
- "Penny made me smile today" survey rate (the north-star signal).
- Qualitative quotes: users naming Penny, printing certificates, returning after 5+ day absences.
- Screenshots of memory in action (Penny referencing user's first lesson, favorite path).

## Slide 8 — Business model (directional, not committed)

- Free forever: hatch, core loop, all base lessons.
- Hodlchi+: cosmetic + emotional depth (outfits, homes, birthdays, deeper Penny personality, second companion). **No pay-to-win, no pay-to-learn.**
- Family / classroom plans (later): shared Penny with individual users.
- Explicitly not: ads, affiliate broker deals, data resale.

## Slide 9 — Market

- Direct: financial-literacy apps (Greenlight, Zogo, Fabric).
- Adjacent: habit + companion apps (Duolingo, Finch, Replika).
- Wedge: the intersection is empty. Habit apps don't teach money; finance apps don't build relationships.

## Slide 10 — Roadmap (next 12 months)

- Sprint 1–2: Memory engine, daily rituals (shipped).
- Sprint 3: Public beta at 500 users, "Penny made me smile" as headline metric.
- Sprint 4: Milestone ceremonies + birthdays.
- Sprint 5: Cohort scale to 5k, Spanish market (already localized).
- Sprint 6: Hodlchi+ tier, measure willingness-to-pay through Penny cosmetics.

## Slide 11 — Team

- Founder(s), relevant background, why-us.
- Note: Hodlchi shipped from concept → private beta → analytics-instrumented → localized in Spanish, iterating with GPT-based agent assistance. That build velocity is part of the moat.

## Slide 12 — Ask

- $ amount, use of funds (weighted toward growth experiments + a single design/animation hire — _not_ more engineers).
- Milestones the round buys: X users, Y D7, first paid tier live.
