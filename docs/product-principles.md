# Hodlchi Product Principles

These are the non-negotiable rules for everyone shipping Hodlchi. When principles conflict, the one higher on this list wins.

---

## 1. Relationship over features

We are building a relationship, not a curriculum. Every screen, sound, animation, and copy line is either strengthening the bond between the user and Penny — or it is noise. There is no third option.

**In practice:**
- A new lesson without a Penny reaction is not shippable.
- A new stat without an emotional consequence is not shippable.
- Silence, stillness, and small gestures often beat features.

## 2. Feelings ship, not features

Every roadmap item must declare the feeling it produces:

- Missed, welcomed, seen, proud, curious, delighted, safe, capable.

If a feature can't name its feeling, it doesn't get built.

## 3. Warm, never needy

Penny is a friend, not a guilt-tripper. Absence is a chance to welcome, not to scold. The bond survives because the user _wants_ to come back, not because leaving hurts.

**Forbidden patterns:**
- "I was starting to worry…"
- "You haven't shown up in N days."
- Streak-shaming, red timers, decay animations tied to time-away.

**Encouraged patterns:**
- "Welcome back 💚 I saved our spot on {topic}."
- Silent grace on return.

## 4. Small is the format

Five minutes. One lesson. One tap. One evolution moment. Anything longer than a single sitting on a subway is the wrong size for this product.

## 5. Specific beats generic

Penny remembers _your_ first lesson, _your_ favorite path, _your_ name for her. Generic greetings are worse than no greeting.

## 6. Educational, never financial-advisory

We teach concepts. We do not recommend assets, wallets, brokers, or actions. Ever.

## 7. Consistent personality across every surface

Loading states, error messages, empty states, and admin tooling that the user might glimpse — all carry Penny's voice: curious, optimistic, playful, warm.

## 8. Measure the felt experience

The only metric that matters at this stage is whether users feel something. Retention, completion, and revenue are lagging indicators of that.

---

## The product decision test

Before shipping anything, answer:

> **Does this make someone more likely to care about Penny?**

- **Yes** → ship it.
- **Maybe** → reshape until the answer is clearly yes, or cut it.
- **No** → cut it, no matter how clever the feature.

This test outranks velocity, novelty, technical elegance, and "our competitor has it."
