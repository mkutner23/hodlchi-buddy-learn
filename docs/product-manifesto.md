# Hodlchi Product Manifesto

## One sentence

Every feature should make Penny feel more alive **or** help users feel more confident with money.

If it does neither, it is not a priority.

---

## What "alive" means

Penny is alive when users feel she is present, personal, and remembering.

- **Memory:** She recalls first lessons, streaks, birthdays, favorite topics, and last visits.
- **Personality:** She has opinions, preferences, and a consistent voice that reacts to the user.
- **Emotion:** She shows joy, curiosity, pride, and welcome—never guilt, pressure, or manipulation.
- **Presence:** She does small things on her own: idle stretches, glances, sighs, and greetings that fit the time of day.

A feature that makes Penny feel more alive increases the odds that someone checks on her in the morning.

## What "confidence" means

Confidence is the feeling that money is no longer confusing or intimidating.

- **Small wins:** Every lesson ends with a clear, achievable step forward.
- **Real-world relevance:** Skills translate to decisions users actually face.
- **No shame:** Mistakes are framed as learning, not failure.
- **Progress you can see:** XP, evolution, and streaks are visible evidence of growth.

A feature that builds confidence makes someone more likely to finish the next lesson and talk about Penny by name.

---

## The filter

Before any feature is added, it must answer one simple question:

> **Does this make someone more likely to care about Penny?**

This is the single product decision test. If the answer is no, cut it or reshape it until the answer is yes.

The two ways to pass the test are:

1. Make Penny feel more alive.
2. Help the user feel more confident with money.

A great feature does both.

---

## Forbidden work

We do not build features that:

- Add complexity without improving the relationship with Penny.
- Gamify through guilt, streak-shaming, or fear of missing out.
- Treat finance as a performance leaderboard.
- Introduce trading, wallets, brokers, or investment recommendations.
- Optimize retention metrics without first improving the underlying feeling.

---

## North Star

> **Help people feel more confident about money—one small win at a time.**

The README attracts. The manifesto focuses. The product earns the love.

Ship. Learn. Improve the product.
