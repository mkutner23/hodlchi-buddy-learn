# Hodlchi Strategy — v1

_Last updated: 2026-07-19_

## Category

Hodlchi is not a finance app with a mascot. It is a **relationship product that produces financial confidence as a side effect**.

Competitors sell knowledge (courses, lessons, drills). We sell a bond with someone who becomes more capable because the user does. Knowledge is commoditized. The relationship is not.

## Positioning (locked)

> Hodlchi is a virtual pet whose life gets better as your financial life gets better.

Sub-line: _5-minute lessons. Daily habits. A companion that grows with you._

## Mission

Help people feel more confident about money — one small win at a time.

## North Star

**% of users who say "Penny made me smile today"** (or equivalent felt-warmth signal), tracked weekly.

Supporting metrics (in priority order):
1. **D1 retention** — did the emotional hook take?
2. **D7 retention** — did the relationship compound?
3. **Weekly active pet-tap rate** — proxy for care, not just usage.
4. Hatch → first lesson conversion.
5. Streak-continuation rate after a missed day.

Revenue, DAU, lesson-completion, and social shares are **downstream**. Do not optimize them directly.

## Moat

Emotional relationships don't ship in a competitor's next release. A user who has raised Penny for 60 days across a streak, three evolutions, and a personal name **cannot be cold-migrated** to a rival product. The switching cost is grief, not friction.

Every design decision should widen that moat.

## What we are NOT

- Not a broker, wallet, or investment product.
- Not a course platform. Lessons are a mechanic, not the product.
- Not a general-purpose AI chatbot. Penny has one job: care about you back.
- Not gamification-on-top-of-education. The pet is the product; education is how the pet grows.

## Guardrails

- Educational only. No trading, wallets, or investment recommendations.
- Penny is warm, curious, playful, optimistic — never guilt-inducing, needy, or manipulative on return.
- No dark patterns for streaks. Absence is met with welcome, not shame.
- Age-agnostic. Copy must work for a 14-year-old and a 54-year-old.

## Roadmap (feelings-first)

Every feature must map to a feeling. If it doesn't, cut it.

| Phase | Feeling to produce | Features |
|-------|--------------------|----------|
| 1 — Make Penny impossible to forget | Missed, remembered, welcomed | Memory engine, absence-aware greetings, milestone recall |
| 2 — Give every day a reason to open | Curious, anticipated | Daily quest, time-of-day rituals, small surprises |
| 3 — Instrument the journey | (internal — clarity) | Funnel events, D1 headline, cohort filtering by invite |
| 4 — Prove the emotional loop | Warmth, delight | "Penny made me smile" micro-survey, sentiment tracking |
| 5 — Deepen the bond | Proud, seen | Milestone rituals, birthdays, evolution ceremonies |
| 6 — Public beta | Belonging | Onboarding polish, referral through Penny (not user) |

## Product decision test

Before shipping anything, ask:

> **Does this make someone more likely to care about Penny?**

If no, cut it or reshape it until the answer is yes.
